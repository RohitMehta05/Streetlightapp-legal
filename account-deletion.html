Account Deletion

How to request deletion of your StreetlightApp account and personal data

Last Updated: 12 September 2026

Your Right to Delete

You have the right to request deletion of your StreetlightApp account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains the available methods, what happens to your data, how long it takes, and what (if anything) we retain afterward.

How to Request Deletion

📱 In-App (Recommended)

Open StreetlightApp → go to Menu → Settings → Personal Info → select Delete Account, and confirm your request. You may be asked to re-enter your password or verify via OTP for security.

✉️ Email Request

Send a deletion request from your registered email address to our support team, including your registered username or phone number for verification.

📞 Phone Support

Deletion Process

  1. Submit your deletion request via the App, email, or phone as described above.
  2. We verify your identity (via OTP, registered email, or account credentials) to prevent unauthorized deletion.
  3. If you are the sole administrator of a Client Organization, we may confirm reassignment of organization data/ownership before proceeding, so operational streetlight/gateway records are not orphaned.
  4. We process the deletion of your personal account data within 30 days of a verified request.
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and associated personal data have been deleted.

What May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period even after account deletion, where necessary for legitimate business, legal, security, or record-keeping purposes:

DataReason for Retention
Streetlight, gateway, ward, and installation records you created on behalf of a client organizationThese are organizational/municipal infrastructure records, not personal data, and remain part of the client organization's operational history.
Transaction, support, or communication logsLegal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention purposes.
Aggregated or anonymized usage dataNo longer identifies you personally and is used for analytics/product improvement.
Backup copiesMay persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being purged in the normal backup rotation cycle.
Data that is retained for legal or operational reasons (as described above) is disassociated from your personal identity wherever possible and is not used for any further active processing.

Important Notes

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to your profile, saved settings, and personal history within the App. If you are part of an organization, deleting your personal account does not delete the organization's streetlight/gateway records — only your individual access and profile data.
  • If you only wish to stop receiving notifications or temporarily stop using the App, you do not need to delete your account — you can log out or adjust notification settings instead.
  • If you are an Installer or Team Member, please coordinate with your organization's Administrator, as they may need to reassign your pending tasks before deletion.
