Security Practices

  • ✔ Data is encrypted in transit between the App and our servers using HTTPS/TLS.
  • ✔ Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms.
  • ✔ Authentication tokens are stored securely on-device and are cleared upon logout or account deletion.
  • ✔ Access to organizational data is restricted by role (Admin, Client Organization, Team Member, Installer).
  • ✔ We can review and respond to data deletion requests, as described on our Account Deletion page.
  • ✔ We do not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Independent Security Review

StreetlightApp has not yet undergone a formal independent third-party security audit. We continuously review and improve our internal security practices and will update this page if that status changes.

Your Controls

  • Manage or revoke Location, Camera, and Photo Library permissions anytime from your device's app settings.
  • Edit or update your personal information from the Settings → Personal Info screen in the App.
  • Request full account and data deletion at any time — see Account Deletion.
  • Contact us with any data-related question at support.hbeonlabs@gmail.com.
